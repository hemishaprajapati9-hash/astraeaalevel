ASTRAEA PWA starter files

Upload these THREE files to the ROOT of your GitHub Pages repository:
- manifest.json
- icon-192.png
- icon-512.png

Then add these two lines inside the <head> section of index.html:
<link rel="manifest" href="./manifest.json">
<meta name="theme-color" content="#0F1221">

Do not replace the whole index.html. Add the two lines near the existing <title>ASTRAEA</title> line, before </head>.
Keep your current index.html and make a backup before editing.
